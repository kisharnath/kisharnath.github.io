🌿 Betel Leaf (Paan) Plant Fast Growth Secret

Grow a healthy and fast-growing Paan (Betel Leaf) plant using a fruit plastic crate and organic layering method.

🧪 Ingredients Used

  • 🍚 Rice Straw
  • 🍂 Dry Leaves
  • 🍌 Banana Stem Pieces
  • 🌱 Mixed Garden Soil
  • ♻️ Compost
  • 🐄 Cow Manure
  • 🐐 Goat Manure
  • 💧 Water

📝 Method

  1. Take a fruit plastic crate.
  2. Add rice straw as the first layer.
  3. Add dry leaves.
  4. Add chopped banana stem pieces.
  5. Add garden soil.
  6. Add compost.
  7. Add cow manure and goat manure.
  8. Mix lightly.
  9. Plant the Betel Leaf (Paan) plant.
  10. Water properly and keep in partial shade.

✅ Benefits

  • Stronger root development
  • Better moisture retention
  • Improved soil fertility
  • Healthy leaf production
  • Faster plant growth
